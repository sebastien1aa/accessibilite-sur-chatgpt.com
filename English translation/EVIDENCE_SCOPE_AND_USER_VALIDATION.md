# Scope of observations and user validation

Detailed native-site feedback from 7 October updates the announcements and behavior in the four Points. Previously validated adapted flows remain accepted. The [versions and confirmations](ENVIRONMENT.md) define the reproduction context.

## Assistant heading and reasoning

With GPT-5.6, the assistant heading appears only after thinking. With **GPT-6 using High reasoning effort**, completed turns and subsequent observations show intermediate commentary headings; these do not guarantee a speaker landmark from the start. In both renderings, the current activity precedes the completed details in JAWS navigation. The [completed-turn contract](Point%202%20-%20Reading%20and%20messages/evidence/2026-10-07-reasoning-contract-gpt6.json), [observed regions](Point%202%20-%20Reading%20and%20messages/evidence/2026-10-08-reasoning-regions.json) and positive user validation are presented in [2B](Point%202%20-%20Reading%20and%20messages/REPORT.md#2b--locating-the-response-and-navigating-reasoning).

## Selection and copying

Ordinary selection is confirmed to work as long as a reading-position jump does not interrupt it. Continuity of navigation and selection in long conversations remains the accessibility issue. Including speakers, durations and timestamps in copied text is a separate product request. The dossier does not promise exact complete copying of any length.

## Other limitations that affect interpretation

The case of numerous analysis cards remains tied to the available example and its reproducibility. The need for intentional access to prompt history remains. Names, roles and DOM relationships are distinguished from announcements and reading positions actually reported.

Technical evidence retains its dates; user transcriptions prioritize the labels relevant to the report. The final check before publication may change an observation if the product evolves.
